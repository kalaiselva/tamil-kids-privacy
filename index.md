# Privacy Policy: தமிழ் எழுத்துகள் (Tamil Letters for Kids)

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

This app helps young children learn the Tamil alphabet. It is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we").
This policy explains what information the app handles. It is written for parents and guardians.

## The short version

- The app does **not** ask for, collect or store any personal information about your child or you:
  no names, no email addresses, no photos, no location, no contacts, no voice recordings.
- There are **no accounts** and **no sign-in**.
- The app shows **ads from Google AdMob**. These ads are set up for children: they are **not personalised**,
  they are limited to **G-rated** (general audience) content, and the app does **not** use the advertising ID.

## What stays on the device

- **Quiz scores** (the best number of stars) and **letter recordings** made by a grown-up in the tracing
  screen's hidden recording mode are saved only on the device, in the app's private storage. They are never
  sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
- **Spoken letters** use the phone's own text-to-speech engine. The app sends it only the Tamil letter or word
  to speak.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows a small banner ad on the menu and on the letter screens, never on the tracing or quiz screens.
Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and the app tells AdMob that every request comes from an app for children:

- every ad request is marked as directed to children, so Google treats it under its policies for children
  and laws such as COPPA (USA) and GDPR (EU);
- ad personalisation is switched off: no ads based on interests or past activity;
- only ads rated **G** (suitable for all ages) are allowed;
- the app does not request the advertising ID, and does not use Android's ad-tracking features
  (ad ID, attribution or interest topics).

To show an ad, AdMob needs some technical information, such as the device's IP address, device type,
operating-system version and the app's version, and it may use it to prevent fraud and measure how ads
perform. Google handles this as described in its policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- AdMob and families: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: only so that ads can load. The lessons, tracing and quiz work without
  an internet connection.

## Children

The app is designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If you believe information about your child has been collected, contact us and we will help.
